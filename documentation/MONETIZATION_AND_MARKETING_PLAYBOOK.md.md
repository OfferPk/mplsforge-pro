\# MPLSForge Pro \- Commercialization & Monetization Playbook

\#\# 1\. Product Market Fit & Target Customer Persona

\#\#\# Primary Audience:  
\- \*\*Telecom & Tier-1/2 ISP Backbone Engineers\*\*: Managing core optical transport, multi-vendor MPLS rings, and LDP/IS-IS/OSPF peering.  
\- \*\*5G Mobile Backhaul & Fronthaul Architects\*\*: Deploying Segment Routing (SR-MPLS / SRv6) and EVPN across distributed cell site routers (CSR).  
\- \*\*Data Center Interconnect (DCI) & Cloud WAN SREs\*\*: Managing spine-leaf fabrics with high-speed 100G/400G backbone links subject to ECMP polarization.  
\- \*\*Enterprise Financial Network Architects\*\*: Maintaining ultra-low-latency WAN links with strict DiffServ QoS and zero packet drop SLAs.

\#\#\# Core Value Proposition:  
"Eliminate silent MTU packet drops, LDP-IGP convergence outages, and ECMP polarization across multi-vendor carrier backbones before deploying to production."
